The thread

The CV looks scattered. It isn't.

International relations, then Japanese, then venture creation. Consulting, sports marketing, operations, digital product. Different rooms, same job: work out what the actual problem is, find the opening, get the people and the pieces together, and make the thing exist.

Here is one whole version of that, start to finish — three months at a company most people have never heard of.

01

Understand the problem

Before changing anything at Shanghai Suoao, I reviewed 20,000+ promotional websites and produced or checked 5,000+ templates.

02

Find the opening

The bottleneck wasn't the people doing the reviews. It was that nobody had written down what "correct" meant.

03

Organise the resources

I wrote the standard operating document: what to check, in what order, and what happens when a template fails.

04

Ship it

Two days of work became two hours. Same team, same tools, a different method.
